# Approved Fluxo visual direction

- Welcome: vivid emerald gradient, overlapping glossy cards, prominent green CTA.
- Home: midnight-teal banking interior, luminous emerald virtual card, prominent balance, Receive/Send, AI Insights, trend chart and pill mobile navigation.
- Monthly payments: dark-teal balance tiles, upcoming bills and quick transfers.
- Consistent dark interiors, emerald primary actions, legible forms, accessible modal surfaces.

Palette: midnight teal `#061B1B`; deep forest `#092C2B`; surface `#103B37`; raised `#194840`; emerald `#008B65`; action green `#28DB90`; mint `#73F5B6`; primary text `#F2FFF9`; secondary text `#A9C9C2`; expense coral `#FF6680`.

Avoid blanket overrides that break contrast on light cards, dialogs or select options. Verify against the original screenshots before claiming an exact match.
